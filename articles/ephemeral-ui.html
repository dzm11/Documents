CASE STUDY Ephemeral UI · for Omnicom SaaS

UI the AI builds for one decision, then dismisses.

Ephemeral UI is best understood not as a prettier overlay, but as an interface the system assembles for a single intent. A media planner shouldn't hunt through dashboards — Omnicom SaaS reads the campaign context and surfaces exactly the slice of UI that's useful right now, lets them act, then gets out of the way. 00 — Concept

What ephemeral UI is.

Ephemeral UI is an interface the AI creates or reveals in response to a concrete intent, and removes once that intent is served. Temporary, contextual, generated on demand.

The sources describe ephemeral UI as an interface generated on demand, temporary, and dependent on the user's context. In the wider generative UI trend, AI produces more than a block of text — it produces interactive tools, panels, and flows fitted to a task.[1]

The shape of it

An ephemeral component appears for a reason, lets you act, and then disappears — leaving a durable trace behind it. If it lingers with no purpose, it has stopped being ephemeral and started being clutter.

01 — The product

Omnicom SaaS, in one layer.

Omnicom SaaS is a single AI-driven media intelligence platform covering campaign optimization, audience & data activation, and media planning. It ships to different teams under different brand names — but it's one product.

Ships as Omnicom SaaS Interact IPG Mediabrands Kinesso

So don't design one giant “AI chat” in the corner — that's the common mistake. Design an AI layer: intelligent, contextual elements that surface across the planning, buying, and activation surfaces the platform already has.

Base SaaS UI
Campaign dashboards, delivery tables, audiences, reportsThe normal platform. It doesn't go away.
AI context layer
Insights, pacing flags, recommendations, quick actionsIntelligence woven into the existing surfaces.
Ephemeral UI layer
Temporary panels, generated reallocations, alerts, mini-flowsAssembled on demand, dismissed after use.
Outcome layer
Applied optimizations, saved audiences, decision logThe durable trace every ephemeral action leaves.

05 — Step three

Design components, not screens.

Across all four scenarios there are only five building blocks. The same parts recombine for every trigger — that's what keeps an AI layer coherent instead of a pile of one-off popups.

A · AI Insight Card

The small card that appears in place: title, short explanation, confidence and source, a primary action, a secondary action, dismiss. Pacing, channel, and audience scenarios all reuse it.

B · Explanation Panel

Opens from an insight: problem, evidence, possible cause, recommended action, CTA. It earns trust by showing its working — essential when the AI is about to move spend.

C · Generated Action Form

A form the AI produces for one action only — a reallocation, an audience swap, a pause. Pre-filled from the recommendation; not the permanent editor.

D · Confidence / Source Chip

Every insight states what it's based on — Omni + Kinesso, a DSP, a verification partner — and how sure it is. Research on AI SaaS UX repeatedly stresses trust, output variability, and the need to explain what the AI is doing.[2]

Based on last 7 days Confidence: High Source: Omni + Kinesso

E · Outcome Toast

After an action the UI disappears, but the planner still knows what happened — with a way to view the result or undo it. Especially important for destructive actions like pausing spend.

07 — Craft

Visual direction.

You don't need futuristic glassmorphism. For a media platform a clean, enterprise style works better: soft cards, subtle shadows, rounded corners, compact panels, small status chips, light motion, clear CTAs, muted AI iconography.

Base UI

Calm, dense, tabular. This is where planners work — keep it quiet.

→
AI & ephemeral layer

A little softer and more contextual, lightly distinguished — visible, but never blocking the work. Risk states earn a warning accent.

The goal: AI elements that are noticeable but not pushy. The base platform stays neutral; the AI layer earns a touch of warmth and a subtle accent, so the planner always knows which parts are the machine talking.

08 — Practice

How to start, step by step.

A path you can follow in an afternoon. Pick narrow, go deep, resist the urge to design the whole platform.

01Pick a surface

Choose one part of Omnicom SaaS.

Campaign optimization, audience & data activation, or media planning. Campaign optimization is the strongest starting point — the pacing scenario reads instantly to any planner.

02Pick a situation

Take a single moment.

Not the whole product — one situation: “the planner wants to know why CTV ROAS dropped.”

03Decision logic

Write the AI's reasoning.

Input: spend, impressions, ROAS, frequency, viewability, channel, audience. Detects: CTV ROAS dropped. Explains: frequency too high, creative fatiguing. Suggests: cap frequency, shift to OLV. UI: insight card + explanation panel + reallocation.

04Components

Design the minimum set.

Insight Card, Explanation Panel, Generated Action Form, Outcome Toast, Decision Log Item. Five components carry all four scenarios.

05Prototype

Wire one flow.

Overview → Insight → Explanation → Action → Confirmation. Show appearance and disappearance, not just static frames.

06Rules

Write the rules last.

When the AI may interrupt. When the UI should disappear. When the planner must confirm. When a result is saved. When an action is destructive enough to need a warning.

First Figma board

Make your opening frame show the mechanism, not just the UI — context on the left, AI interpretation in the middle, the generated response on the right. It tells the client this is a logical AI system, not a random popup.

09 — The principle

The one rule.

In Omnicom SaaS, every ephemeral component should answer three questions. If it can't, it's a decorative AI element, not good ephemeral UI.

It must answer

Why am I seeing this?

What should I do next?

What happens after I act?

AI detects a signal → shows an insight → explains the cause → proposes an action → creates an outcome. The arc behind all four scenarios

Get that arc right once — the pacing flow is the clearest — and the rest of the AI layer is just more instances of the same idea, whether it ships as Omnicom SaaS, Interact, IPG Mediabrands, or Kinesso.
